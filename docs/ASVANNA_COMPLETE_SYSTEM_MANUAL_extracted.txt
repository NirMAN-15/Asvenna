1. Plain-Language Executive Summary (For Non-IT Persons &
Farmers)
In Sri Lanka's central hill country (Bandarawela, Welimada, Nuwara Eliya, Badulla), vegetable farming
families suffer massive recurring financial losses. On average, 
30% to 40% of their perishable crop
output is wasted annually, amounting to over Rs. 180 Billion in national post-harvest loss. 
The core driver of this crisis is  
"Trend Planting" (වගා රැල්ල). When leeks or cabbage prices surge at
wholesale  economic  centers  (Dambulla,  Manning  Market,  Keppetipola),  hundreds  of  farmers
simultaneously cultivate the exact same crop. Because these vegetables take 60–90 days to mature,
thousands of tons reach harvest in the exact same week. This floods the market, causing prices to crash
below harvesting and transport costs (e.g., Leeks crashing from Rs. 350/kg down to Rs. 20/kg).

🌟 Five Foundational Ecosystem Pillars:
• Pillar 1: Regional Cultivation Monitoring: GPS-tagged plotting of active cultivation across
agrarian divisions.
• Pillar 2: Predictive Risk Engine: Evaluates supply density against national demand (CROPIX)
with 3 tiers: SAFE <70% , AT-RISK 70–85% , OVER-PLANTED >85% .
• Pillar 3: Smart Crop Recommendations: 4-factor composite weighting (Market Gap 35%, Soil
25%, Weather 20%, Historical Price 20%).
• Pillar 4: Geo-Fenced Marketplace: 5 km radius direct trade connecting farmers with local
caterers, hoteliers, and bulk buyers.
• Pillar 5: Trilingual Accessibility: Native Sinhala (සිංහල), Tamil (்்்்்), and English localization
across mobile and web interfaces.
